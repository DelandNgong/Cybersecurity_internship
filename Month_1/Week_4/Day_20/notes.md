Day 20: Week 4 Review & Presentation

#Weekly Summary: Mastering the Incident Response Lifecycle

This week focused on the end-to-end process of handling a cybersecurity incident, from the initial framework to real-world application.

#Key Topics Covered:
1. Incident Response Lifecycle: Studied the 6 core stages: Preparation, Detection & Analysis, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, and Post-Incident Activity.
2. Reporting & Documentation: Learned the critical components of a professional incident report and created a sample report for a malware intrusion.
3. Real-World Case Studies: Analyzed a Business Email Compromise (BEC) attack and a government web app hack to see the IR lifecycle in action.
4. The Human Element: Researched the vital role of Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) in a successful incident response.

#Core Skills Developed:
-   Understanding how to structure a coordinated response to a security incident.
-   Technical writing and communication skills for documenting incidents.
-   Analytical skills for dissecting real-world attacks and response strategies.

#Presentation Focus:
The weekly presentation will summarize this journey from IR theory to practical application, highlighting the created sample report and key lessons from the case studies.
